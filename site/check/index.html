Check a car

Check a car before you buy it

Type the registration. Each button copies it and opens the official check in a new tab, where you paste it. This site sends the registration nowhere.

The free official checks

  • Vehicle details and tax status (DVLA)Make, colour, fuel, engine size, first registration, tax and MOT status. Compare them with the advert and the logbook (V5C).Copy and open
  • MOT history (DVSA)Tests since 2005 in England, Scotland and Wales, and since 2017 in Northern Ireland: dates, mileage, results and advisories. Look for mileage that goes down or gaps, and repeated advisories on rust, brakes or tyres.Copy and open
  • London charges (TfL)Whether ULEZ applies to this exact car: use TfL's answer, not a guess from its age. The Congestion Charge applies in central London on top, whatever the car.Copy and open
  • Recalls (DVSA)Any safety recall on the make and model, and whether it was fixed.Copy and open
  • The dealer, if it is a company (Companies House)The company's status and details. It is not a quality guarantee.Copy and open

Not in the free checks

Outstanding finance, reported theft and insurance write-offs are in paid history checks from commercial providers. Check which databases a report covers and when it was run. No report shows every past accident or repair, and none replaces matching the VIN on the car to the V5C. (GOV.UK.) More in What a plate tells you.
