Each source this site points to, read on 23 November 2026. Check the terms before reusing any of them.

SourcePublisherWhat it givesIts limits
Vehicle enquiry serviceDVLAMake, colour, fuel, engine, first registration, tax and MOT status, from the registration.No owner, finance or accident history.
MOT historyDVSATest dates, mileage, failures and advisories.A test is not a full condition report.
Vehicle checkerTfLWhether ULEZ and other London charges apply to a vehicle.London only; rates change, see TfL.
Vehicle recall checkDVSASafety recalls on a make and model.Ask the seller whether the work was done.
DVLA Vehicle Enquiry Service APIDVLARegistration in, vehicle facts out (JSON). Application and API key; a default rate limit for new applications.Not finance, ownership or accident history; fields may be missing.
MOT history APIDVSATest results, mileage, defects and advisories for cars, motorcycles and vans in Great Britain since 2005. Registration, OAuth client credentials and an API key, kept on a server.Tests are not a full condition report or accident history.
Vehicle licensing statisticsDfT and DVLAPublished tables of the fleet by fuel, age, area, body type and CO2 band.Aggregates, not individual cars.
Road safety open data (STATS19)DfTPolice-recorded personal-injury collisions, vehicles and casualties in Great Britain.Not a search of one car's crash history; excludes unreported and damage-only collisions.
Police dataPolice.ukRecorded crime by area, including vehicle crime; monthly CSV and an API.Locations are anonymised; not one car's theft history.
Company registerCompanies HouseCompany status, registered details and filings; public search and a developer API.Identity, not honesty, solvency or quality.
Insurance database checkMIBWhether a vehicle shows as insured, for vehicles you own or may legally drive.Not a public lookup, and not proof that you are covered.
Consumer Rights Act 2015legislation.gov.ukYour rights when you buy from a trader, including the short-term right to reject.A private sale gives fewer protections.

Guidance: GOV.UK, checks when buying a used car; GOV.UK, vehicle insurance; GOV.UK, tax your vehicle; GOV.UK, sold or bought a vehicle; Citizens Advice, buying a used car.

This site calls none of these from your browser. A later version may fetch DVLA and MOT data for you, with your own keys; that will be a separate, stated choice.
