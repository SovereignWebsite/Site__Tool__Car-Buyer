The one thing to remember

Buying the car, telling DVLA, taxing it and insuring it are separate steps. Before you drive it away you need insurance that covers you, your own vehicle tax (tax does not pass to the buyer), a valid MOT where one is required, the right licence, and a roadworthy car. In London, ULEZ (£12.50 a day for a car that is not compliant) and the Congestion Charge (£18 a day in the central zone) are on top, as TfL published them on 23 November 2026; see Costs. (GOV.UK, checks; GOV.UK, tax; TfL, ULEZ.)
