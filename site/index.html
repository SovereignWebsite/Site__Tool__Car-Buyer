Buying a used car in the UK

Find out as much as you can about a car before you pay for it, and drive it home legally. The free checks here are official services, a paid history report is the one thing you buy, and everything you type stays in your browser.

  • Costs

  • Sources

The one thing to remember

Buying the car, telling DVLA, taxing it and insuring it are separate steps. Before you drive it away you need insurance that covers you, your own vehicle tax (tax does not pass to the buyer), a valid MOT where one is required, the right licence, and a roadworthy car. In London, ULEZ and the Congestion Charge are on top. (GOV.UK, checks; GOV.UK, tax; TfL, ULEZ.)

Your registration numbers, notes and ticks are saved only in this browser (its local storage), never sent to us. You can export them as a file and delete them at any time. A number plate can be personal data, so keep it that way.
