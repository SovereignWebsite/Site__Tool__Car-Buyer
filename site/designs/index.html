Designs

Five designs

The same facts and tools, laid out five ways. Pick one with "Choose a design" at the top of any page, or open one below; your choice is kept in this browser.

  • 1 Forecourt

    The plain reference, on purpose: one column, a card for each tool, calm green and nothing in the way. The easiest to read, and the default.

  • 2 Logbook

    Numbered fields in boxes, like the V5C logbook, with a document reference line and a serif hand.

  • 3 MOT certificate

    A notice box first, then the checks as test items with a result for each, like the test certificate.

  • 4 Windscreen card

    Big figures first (your registration, when it was issued, the first year's cost), then a short list, like a forecourt price card.

  • 5 Road atlas

    The purchase as a numbered route, junction by junction, with a map key: official, paid, in your browser.

Design 1, Forecourt, is the one we picked for the home page: it keeps the checklist calm and readable, which the reviews said mattered most.
