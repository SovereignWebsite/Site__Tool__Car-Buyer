What you can do here yourself

This site is plain files in site/ on the dev branch. Edit any page on GitHub in the browser, or ask your own Claude (Use with your AI). The checks run on every change and publish within minutes. Nothing here waits on anyone.

  • Lift the draft notice: delete the line that starts <p class="draft"> in site/index.html.
  • Change a fact or a rate: each sits beside its source; the London rates are also in site/assets/app.js (ULEZ, CC).
  • Add or reorder checklist items: site/checklist/index.html, one line per item.
  • Pick the default design: change '1' in site/assets/design.js.
  • Add a photo you own: put it in site/assets/ and reference it with a credit.
