Before you commit

At the viewing

An MOT pass does not test the engine, clutch or gearbox. (GOV.UK; Citizens Advice.)

On the day you pay

Paying safely, and private sales

  • Get the original log book (V5C) and the MOT document, not photocopies. Never buy a car without the log book.
  • Meet a private seller at their home, so you have a record of their address if something goes wrong.
  • Cash has no fees, but none of the protection some card or credit payments give, and carrying it can be unsafe. A debit card may have a chargeback scheme. Finance arranged by a trader costs more, but can give you extra protection.
  • From a private seller you have fewer rights than from a trader. Under the Consumer Rights Act 2015 a trader's car must be of satisfactory quality, fit for purpose and as described.
  • Paid history reports (finance, theft, write-offs) are commercial services: check which databases a report covers before you rely on it.

Source: Citizens Advice, buying a used car, read 23 November 2026.
