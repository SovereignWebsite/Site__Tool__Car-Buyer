Before you commit

At the viewing

View in daylight, ideally when the car is dry. Ask for a cold start. Check warning lights, leaks, smoke, tyres, brakes, steering, rust and every electrical item, and both keys. Ask for service invoices, not only stamps. An MOT pass does not test the engine, clutch or gearbox, so an independent inspection is worth it. (GOV.UK, Citizens Advice.)
